Voxellax

Two selfie videos in, a chunky 3D voxel video out. Play it back with your head moving the view.

How to record
  1. Start a selfie video on the tablet held in your left hand.
  2. Start a selfie video on the phone held in your right hand.
  3. Say hello, smile, move things around. Keep your face in both shots.
  4. Stop both videos and upload them here. The sound is used to line them up.

Everything is processed on this device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Results are rough and noisy: depth is guessed by an AI model from each frame.
